Account deletion

Public request page for Website CRM account and associated personal account data deletion. No login is required to view these instructions.

This page is for Website CRM (mobile and web). It explains how to delete your Website CRM login account and what happens to data.

1. Delete from the Website CRM mobile app (recommended when signed in)

If you can still open Website CRM and sign in:

  1. Open Website CRM on your device.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Choose Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the irreversible deletion prompt.

Authenticated deletion permanently removes your ability to sign in and irreversibly de-identifies your personal account fields on the Website CRM servers.

2. Request deletion without the app (web / email)

If you cannot use the in-app flow (for example you uninstalled the app), request deletion by contacting the privacy contact below. Include:

  • Your Website CRM login ID (do not send passwords).
  • Your full name as shown on the account, if known.
  • Subject line: Website CRM Account Deletion Request.

Privacy contact: [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]
Support contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]

After identity verification by the operator, the same account-deletion process used by the authenticated API is applied. This page does not delete accounts automatically from a public form, and nobody can delete an arbitrary account by entering only an email address here.

3. What is deleted or irreversibly de-identified

  • Personal account name (replaced with “Deleted User”).
  • Login ID (replaced with a unique deleted identifier).
  • Password hash (replaced with an unusable value).
  • Active account status (account deactivated).
  • Project assignments for that user.
  • Mobile session tokens and matching web sessions (signed out everywhere).

4. What may be retained (business / audit)

  • Shared projects and Google Sheets configuration.
  • Lead rows stored in Google Sheets (owned by the spreadsheet / Google account holders).
  • Remarks and timeline history needed for CRM audit continuity (authors may display as “Deleted User”).
  • Shared Google Sheets authorization used by Website CRM administrators (not removed by deleting a CRM login; use Disconnect Google on the web admin screen to clear credentials).

Temporary freezing alone is not used as a substitute for deletion of personal account data. Personal identifiers on the account record are irreversibly removed or replaced. Account deletion does not delete Google Sheets, shared project lead rows, or other users’ accounts.

5. Final administrator

The last active administrator cannot delete their own account until another administrator exists, so the organization is not left without administrative access. Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL] if you need help transferring administration before deletion.
